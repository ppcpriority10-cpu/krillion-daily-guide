Krillion Daily Guide — How to Play & Score Rare Answers

Krillion flips trivia on its head: the obvious answer is the worst answer. Here is how the game works, how scoring really functions, and five habits that will take your dives deeper.

What Is Krillion?

Krillion is a free daily trivia game built on one unusual idea — rarity beats knowledge. Every day you get a fresh set of prompts. For each one you type a single answer, and your score depends on how few other players gave the same answer. The player who thinks the rarest thoughts wins the deepest dive.

The whole loop takes about three minutes a day, which is exactly why it caught on: it is a daily ritual, not a time sink. If you have never played, Dive Krillion is a free companion site where you can learn the ropes and practice.

How to Play: The Basics

A daily dive is simple on the surface:

RuleDetail
Prompts per dive7 naming prompts (e.g. "name a red fruit")
Time per prompt25 seconds — one answer, no do-overs
AttemptsOne dive per day on the official game
GoalGive the answer fewest players will think of
ScoringRarity tiers, 10–100 points per answer

The timer is not there to reward fast typing — it exists to stop you from looking things up. The 25 seconds are thinking time. Your first idea is almost always everyone's first idea, which is why it scores badly. The points live in your third or fourth idea. For the full rules breakdown, see how to play Krillion.

The 6 Rarity Tiers

Every answer lands in one of six tiers based on how many players gave it. Fewer players, more points:

TierPointsWhat it means
Plankton10The most common answers — everyone's first thought
Minnow15Common, but not the single most popular
Dolphin30Uncommon — you thought past the obvious
Shark60Rare — most players missed this one
Whale85Very rare — a genuinely unusual answer
One in a Krillion100Almost nobody else gave it — the perfect answer

Points convert to depth at 10 metres per point, so a perfect 700-point dive reaches 7,000 metres — the trench floor.

5 Tips for Rarer Answers

1. Ban your first instinct. Whatever pops into your head first is what pops into ten thousand other heads. Deliberately discard it and keep thinking — your second and third ideas score dramatically better.
2. Go specific, not clever. "Paris" is a fine answer; "Lyon" is a better one. Specificity beats cleverness almost every time, because most players stop at the famous example.
3. Study the archive. Past dives reveal which answers the crowd overuses. Players who review yesterday's answers start noticing the patterns — and avoiding the traps. The Krillion strategy guide walks through the most common ones category by category.
4. Think in categories, not answers. For "name a dog breed," don't list breeds — think of groups (herding, toy, extinct, regional) and pick from the least obvious group. Structure beats brainstorming.
5. Use the full 25 seconds. There is no speed bonus. Players who answer in 5 seconds are donating points to players who use all 25. Sit with the prompt; let the obvious answers pass through and wait for the rare one underneath.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Krillion free to play?

Yes. The daily dive is completely free with no account required. Practice modes like Krillion Unlimited are free too.

What happens if I skip a prompt?

A skipped prompt scores zero — no tier, no points. A weak guess at least lands in Plankton for 10 points, so it is almost always better to type something than nothing.

Do misspellings count?

Minor typos are usually forgiven by the answer matching, but a badly mangled spelling can be marked wrong. When in doubt, pick the answer you can spell confidently.

How do I get better at thinking rare?

Practice deliberately: review past answers, drill weak categories, and build the habit of discarding your first idea. The Krillion strategy guide covers training methods for all eight categories.
